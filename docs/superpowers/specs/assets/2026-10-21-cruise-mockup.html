How it holds up on phones

  • The dusk sky, stars and waves are a photo with light CSS motion, not a live 3D ocean. That keeps the C mood without draining batteries.
  • The globe is a small, light 3D library (about 5 KB). It loads after the page has shown, so the page never waits for it.
  • The chart is a real map in the brand night colours. You swipe through the days and the map follows.
  • The price and "Ask about this sailing" button stay pinned to the bottom.
  • Older or slow phones, and people who turn off motion, get the same page with still images.

Effort: about 2–3 weeks, between B and C. It needs no 3D ship model.
